                    About the data

                    What the numbers mean

                    • Percentages are shares of the students in the current view who answered the question. They describe respondents only.
                    • “At least sometimes” counts Sometimes, Often and Very often (or Almost every time). “Often” counts the top two answers.
                    • Multi-select questions add up to more than 100%.
                    • Money and spending were reported in ranges. The dashboard shows the median range (the lower middle answer) and never an exact amount.
